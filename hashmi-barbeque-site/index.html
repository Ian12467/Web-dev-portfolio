01 — The place

A table that feels
easy to return to.

Parklands · 3rd Floor

Set on the third floor of Broadwalk Mall on Ojijo Road, the restaurant is presented as a straightforward destination for grilled dishes, breads and cool desserts. The dining experience is built around familiar plates, shared eating and the atmosphere of a busy room.

The public listing notes dine-in, takeaway and no-contact delivery. Customer reviews repeatedly mention naan, grilled meats, Chooza chicken, kebabs and faluda.
